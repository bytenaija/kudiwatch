KudiWatch — clickable mockups

Mobile-first PWA screens, designed at 360 px. Every screen links to the next step of its flow; the striped preview bar on each page jumps anywhere.

These mockups simulate product behavior with inline JS only (fake playback, fake OTP 482916). Money screens carry the mock-mode banner. See DESIGN.md · COPY.md · DECISIONS-DESIGN.md for the system, copy, and deltas vs the engineering deepdive.
